**Her instinct is right. This chart is built for moving work, not desk work.**

- Four systems agree on this. Sitting all day will make her restless and unhappy no matter how well it pays.

### Best fit: hands-on health and body work (three-plus systems agree)
Why:
- Vedic: Scorpio rising, which traditionally points to medicine, healing and research.
- Western: Virgo at the top of the chart, which points to health, service and precision.
- Ziwei: the healing/caretaking star is energized, and the strategy star is highlighted in her health palace.

Examples:

- Sports science or strength coach
- Nurse, especially travel or international nursing
- Occupational therapist
- Paramedic or flight nurse
- Field-based public health

### Second: work that travels
Why:
- Her current 20-year Vedic period (2023–2043) is run by Venus placed in her "foreign lands" house.
- Vedic: Jupiter at peak strength in her "long journeys" house.
- Ziwei: the money star sits in her Travel palace, meaning she earns away from her home base.

### Third: teaching or training on her feet
Speaking and writing are her strongest skills. Instructor or field-trainer roles let her use them while staying mobile.

### The sweet spot
A **licensed, portable, hands-on health credential** that is recognized abroad. It covers all three of her strengths, and it lines up with the 2031–2033 window for moving abroad.

### Avoid
- **Back-office, accounting, data entry.** Her chart does show a knack for managing money, but in a desk job that knack won't make up for how restless she will be.
- **Commission-only income.** She earns in spikes and doesn't naturally hold onto money, so she needs a base salary plus automatic savings.

- The 2026–2029 relationship read depends on an accurate birth time, and 09:03 is close to a cutoff in some traditions.
